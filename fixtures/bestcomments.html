swatcoder 1 day ago | parent | context | on: I'm not convinced the AI bubble exists

Railroads, semiconductors, plastics, networking... the utility was there for all of them, and so was the potential profit.

Everybody knew it.

But some investors don't have the patience for these "sure things" to mature into their commercial destiny and others know they can siphon off a killing by taking advantage of them as they speculate.

These bubbles aren't about potential, they're about timing.

And today's AI, for as revolutionary as it may be, is taking just as long to mature as anything in the past.

Most of the top players involved in AI right now are going to be rich. And in 20 years, the world is going to involve a lot of AI in soooo many things. chimpontherun 10 hours ago | parent | context | on: Locating the source of a mysterious beep

Around '05 or so, the house we were staying in started beeping. Smoke alarm sounds, as usual. For almost a day we couldn't find the source. Contacted the owners -- they didn't know it either. They bought the house few years prior, so not much of a help.

Standing in the hallways listening at different angles, as the article says, didn't help. No Fable in '05 either.

So I had to improvise. Got a ~1 meter stick and two headphones (every headphone is a microphone, even if it sucks as a microphone).
